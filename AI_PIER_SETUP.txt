AI Pier Finder setup
====================

1) Create .env.local in the rebar-planner project root:

OPENAI_API_KEY=your_openai_api_key_here
# Optional:
# OPENAI_PIER_MODEL=gpt-4.1

2) Restart the dev server:

Ctrl+C
npm run dev

3) Upload the PDF and click Extract Plan Data.

4) Open "AI pier finder" and click "Find piers with AI".

The AI result does not automatically overwrite the form. Review the count and then click "Apply AI pier count to form" only if it looks right.
